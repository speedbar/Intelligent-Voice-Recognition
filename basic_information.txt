2. Refund and Return Policies
Refund and Returns Guidelines
Standard Return Window:
John Lewis Stores/Online: Returns accepted within 35 days of purchase.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
Proof of purchase is required.
How to Return Items:
In-store Returns: Bring the item with proof of purchase to any John Lewis store.
Online Purchases:
Use the prepaid returns label in your order.
Options include Royal Mail, Collect+, or courier collections.
Furniture & Large Items: Call the customer support team to arrange a collection.
Refund Processing:
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned item.
Gift Cards: Refunds on items purchased with a gift card will be reissued as a gift card.
Non-Returnable Items:
Perishable goods (e.g., food, flowers).
Personalized and custom-made items.
Hygiene-sensitive products (e.g., opened cosmetics, underwear).
Faulty or Damaged Products:
Faulty items can be returned at any time if under warranty.
Customers can request a repair, replacement, or refund.
Contact Customer Services immediately for assistance.

JLP Refund Policy Overview
1. Return Period
Standard Returns: Customers can return items within 35 days of purchase.
Faulty or Defective Items: Returns accepted anytime during the warranty period.
2. Conditions for Returns
Eligible Items: Products must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all components.
Proof of Purchase: Required, including receipts, e-receipts, or order confirmations.
Assembled Items: Returns for assembled furniture depend on store discretion (must be in good condition).
3. Refund Method
Original Payment Method:
Credit/Debit Card: Refunds issued to the original card.
Cash: Refunds provided in cash.
Gift Cards: Refunds reissued as John Lewis Gift Cards.
PayPal/Apple Pay: Refund sent to the original account.
Refunds typically processed within 5-10 business days.
4. Non-Returnable Items
Personalized or custom-made items (e.g., tailored curtains, monogrammed products).
Perishable food items from Waitrose sections.
Hygiene-sensitive products (e.g., opened cosmetics, underwear, and mattresses).
5. Online Order Returns
Items purchased online can be returned:
In-store (free).
By post using the prepaid return label.
Return Shipping: Charges may apply unless the product is faulty.
6. Assembly and Delivery Charges
Delivery and assembly fees are non-refundable unless the product is defective.
7. Special Considerations
Damaged or Missing Parts: Customers can contact support to replace parts or resolve issues promptly.
John Lewis Membership: "My John Lewis" members may receive extended return windows or exclusive refund benefits.
